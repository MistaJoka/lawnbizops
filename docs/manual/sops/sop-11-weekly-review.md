# SOP-11 — Weekly review

| Trigger                                       | Frequency | Screens                              | Time    |
| --------------------------------------------- | --------- | ------------------------------------ | ------- |
| End of the work week (pairs with billing day) | Weekly    | Dashboard, Money → Reports, Pipeline | ~20 min |

## TL;DR

Dashboard for orientation, Reports for the P&L and job-profitability truth, aging for who owes you, Pipeline counts for whether next month is fed. Every number on the Dashboard is tappable — chase anything surprising to its source. The one rule: **the review ends with three concrete follow-up tasks, not with a feeling.**

## ELI5

Friday night team meeting, except the team is you. Four questions, each with a screen that answers it: Did money come in? (Dashboard) Which lawns actually pay me? (Reports) Who owes me? (Aging) Will there be work next month? (Pipeline) Then you write down the three things Monday-you must do — because Friday-you always overestimates Monday-you's memory.

## Steps

1. **More → Dashboard.** Read the cards: Collected this month, Outstanding, Open pipeline; jobs scheduled/done this week; open follow-ups; clients by stage. **Tap any number that surprises you** — every one drills into its source screen.
2. **Money → Reports**, range = this month:
   - **Profit & loss (cash basis)** — collected minus spent. Is net where the month should be?
   - **Expenses by category** — any category quietly ballooning?
   - **Income by method** — mostly "other"? You're using board shortcuts instead of real payment recording (SOP-08 step 3).
   - **Job profitability (billed)** — best and worst. The worst jobs are a pricing decision waiting for you.
     > **Gap [G-04]:** costs only include what you logged — unlogged materials make bad jobs look fine. Fix the habit (SOP-10), not the report. → [findings](../../ops-manual-findings-2026-07-31.md#g-04)
   - **A/R aging** — anything right of Current feeds SOP-08 tomorrow.
     > **Gap [G-21]:** there's no month-over-month trend or revenue-by-service view — flip the date range to last month and compare by eye, or export CSVs into a spreadsheet for trends. → [findings](../../ops-manual-findings-2026-07-31.md#g-21)
3. **Share PDF** of the report if you keep weekly records — it lands with your logo, ready for a lender or your accountant.
4. **Pipeline glance:** are Lead + Quoted columns fed for next month? Thin pipeline → tonight's task list includes win-back texts (SOP-09 step 5).
5. **Write the three tasks.** On the relevant client (or as standalone follow-ups on Today): "Reprice Hendersons", "Nudge the two 60-day invoices", "Text 5 dormants about fall cleanup" — each with a Monday due date.
   > **Gap [G-08]:** due dates surface only in Today's Follow-ups — which you check every morning (SOP-05), so Monday-you is covered. → [findings](../../ops-manual-findings-2026-07-31.md#g-08)

## Done when

- All five report sections read and surprises chased to source.
- Three dated follow-up tasks exist.
- Billing day (SOP-07) already happened this week — if not, do it first; reviewing unbilled weeks reviews fiction.

## What can go wrong

| Symptom                            | Cause                          | Fix                                                               |
| ---------------------------------- | ------------------------------ | ----------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Report range shows the empty state | No activity in range           | Check the range picker before concluding the month was $0         |
| Collected ≠ what you remember      | Payments not recorded same-day | SOP-08 discipline; reconcile against your bank now                |
| Job profits all look identical     | No expenses/labor logged       | Set your labor rate (Business profile) and log materials (SOP-10) |
